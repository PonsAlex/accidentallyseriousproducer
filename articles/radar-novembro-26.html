Flame Sound - Pyro

Pyro is a cinematic starter kit: 269 effects across eleven categories — braams, impacts, sub drops, risers, whooshes, alarms, glitches, interface sounds, ambience — delivered as 24-bit WAV up to 192 kHz, 3.5 GB, royalty-free. It normally costs $49, and Flame Sound is giving it to UJAM users until the end of November. The code is applied automatically at checkout and no card is needed, only an email address.

Verdict: 269 cinematic effects in eleven categories, 24-bit WAV, free until the end of November for an email address. The radar's first sample pack.

Busy Works Beats - Soft Clipper 3000

Soft Clipper 3000 does the two jobs beatmakers keep reaching for: catch the peaks without squashing the life out of them, and show you what the gain is actually doing while you do it. Busy Works Beats gives it away for an email address. It runs as AU and VST3 on macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel, and as VST3 on 64-bit Windows.

Verdict: A soft clipper and limiter with precision metering. The metering is the reason to keep it; the clipping is a crowded field.

PSP Audioware - ClassicQlite

ClassicQlite is PSP Audioware's take on the classic British equaliser curve, given away outright. Getting it means creating a free account in the PSP user area and having an iLok user ID, which is also free — no purchase and no dongle required. It runs as VST, VST3, AU and AAX on Windows and macOS.

Verdict: A British-style EQ from a house that has been modelling consoles for decades, free for an account and an iLok ID.

Flame Sound - Vatrica

Vatrica is built on the processing engine behind Flame Sound's VATRA: one knob, three modes — MSTR, TAPE and SUBS — to add loudness, warmth, punch, weight or air to a mix without deciding which of a dozen controls to turn. It is free outright, and unusually there is no licence key, no trial and no account: download, install, use. VST3, AU and AAX on Mac and Windows.

Verdict: One knob, three modes, no licence key and no account. The lowest-friction thing on the page.
