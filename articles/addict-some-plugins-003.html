Addict Some Plugins #003

The August 14 Radar

Twelve checked signals. One expired freebie. Zero automatic recommendations.

This is a status desk, not a review. Every item below cleared the ASP intake checklist on August 14, but a confirmed launch or promotion is not the same thing as a verdict on sound, stability or usefulness.

Claim first. Install later. A green checkbox is permission to investigate, not an instruction to collect.

Availability moves quickly. The source links are included so prices, platform support and countdowns can be checked again before acting.

Free and Claimable

SPECTAVERB — TEXTURE

STATUS: FREE. A multi-effect reverb built around three parallel chains for spectral space, movement and colour. The developer currently lists it at $0+.

Developer page →

AEROMOTION — Semedo Audio

STATUS: FREE. A ten-mode modulation effect covering movement, width and depth. The zero-cost order and the ten included modes were confirmed during intake.

ASP note: Claim it only if a multi-mode modulation box has an immediate job in a session.

noshige76 — noshigeSound

STATUS: LIMITED-TIME FREE. The FET-style compressor was listed as free to claim through August 19, 2026, with a perpetual licence for claims made inside the window.

Developer page →

Pigments Play — Arturia

STATUS: FREE. A focused version of Pigments with 100 curated presets, six synthesis types, 20 effects and reduced editing controls.

Official page →

Steinberg Classics

STATUS: FREE. Five early VST names return in one collection: Neon, VB-1, CS-40, LM7 and Karlette. Nostalgia is allowed; pretending a 1999 synth replaces every modern instrument is optional.

Official page →

Whirl DT — Moth Production

STATUS: FREE BETA. A small, independent drop-tuning effect inspired by Whammy DT-style workflows. Beta means useful feedback is welcome and mission-critical sessions should keep a backup plan.

STATUS: ANNOUNCED. An analog-percussion instrument for Kontakt Player built around two sequenced layers and a real-time rhythmic variation engine. Harley Benton Space Wah, Space Volume and Space Wah/Volume

STATUS: NEW RELEASE. Three compact treadle pedals cover wah, volume and the combined job. This is hardware, so it belongs in the radar—not in the plugin download queue.

The ASP Reality Check

A status label is not a verdict.

FREE describes the price observed during verification.

NEW RELEASE and UPDATED describe product activity.

ANNOUNCED means the public release may still be ahead.

None of them means Fire, Stash, Digital Furniture or Nah until ASP actually tests the thing.

Claim what is genuinely useful.

Recheck every timer.

Let betas be betas.

The radar finds the signal. The session decides whether it matters.

— ASP
