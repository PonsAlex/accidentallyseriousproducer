Addict Some Plugins #003

The August 2026 Audit

Small Tools, Short Timers and the Usual Bundle Gravity

August is the month when plugin marketing quietly changes costume. The summer sale language remains, but the inbox starts hinting at back-to-work discipline, studio resets and the extremely optimistic idea that a new compressor will make your autumn more productive.

It might. It probably will not.

This audit uses the same ASP rule as always: useful beats dramatic. A free utility that solves one boring problem is more interesting than a massive bundle that turns your downloads folder into an archaeological site.

The best August opportunities are the ones that make a session easier, not the ones that make a receipt look impressive.

Download This

A simple meter you will actually open

Every producer claims to want more creative tools. Then the mix falls apart because the gain staging is decorative fiction. A clean meter, analyzer or waveform helper is not glamorous, but it can stop a bad decision before it becomes a printed bounce.

A lightweight saturation plugin

Saturation remains the audio software equivalent of hot sauce: useful in moderation, suspicious when applied to everything. The good ones still earn their place by doing one thing quickly and not requiring a thirty-minute identity ceremony.

Consider Carefully

The discounted flagship

A serious discount on a serious plugin can be sensible. The danger is pretending the sale has answered the only question that matters: where will this go in a real project? If the answer is vague, the discount is just decoration.

The almost-free expansion

Preset packs, impulse responses and mini expansions are excellent when they support music you are already making. They are less useful when they become a tiny museum of abandoned intentions.

Politely Ignore

Any bundle that requires a spreadsheet before you know whether you want it deserves suspicion. Value is not the same as volume. Owning more tools is not the same as having a better workflow.

The ASP Reality Check

Would I use this next week?

Not someday. Not during a future cinematic rebrand of your studio. Next week, in an actual session, when the track is messy and the enthusiasm is running out. If a plugin survives that question, it may be worth your attention.

Final Score

Download immediately

  • Useful metering utilities
  • Simple workflow helpers
  • Free tools with no account maze

Consider carefully

  • Discounted flagship processors
  • Small expansions for tools you already use
  • Focused creative effects

Politely ignore

  • Bundles measured by weight
  • Countdowns without a workflow reason
  • Another analog miracle you did not need yesterday
