How it works

A free plugin is not a mandatory installation.

Free plugins appear all week.

ASP downloads, installs and tests them before your plugin folder has to suffer.

Every Friday, the week's freebies are ranked by usefulness, workflow value and the likelihood that they will still be installed next month.

The point is not to collect everything. It is to decide what deserves a real place in the setup, what is worth claiming for later and what can be ignored without emotional damage.

Evaluation criteria

What gets tested

Usefulness
Does it solve a real production problem?
Workflow
Is it fast, clear and practical?
Character
Does it offer something meaningfully different?
Staying Power
Will it still be installed next month?

Four possible outcomes

The ranking

🔥 Fire

Download and install.

Useful, distinctive and likely to remain in the setup.

📦 Stash

Claim it while it is free.

Do not necessarily install it now, but it may be worth keeping in your account for a future project.

🪑 Digital Furniture

It works, but it will probably spend most of its life occupying disk space and appearing in plugin scans.

🚫 Nah

Not worth the registration, download, installation or additional plugin scan.
