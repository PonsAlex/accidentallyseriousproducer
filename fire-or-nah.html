How it works

A free plugin is not a mandatory installation.

Free plugins appear all week.

ASP downloads, installs and tests them before your plugin folder has to suffer.

Every Friday, the week's freebies are ranked by usefulness, workflow value and the likelihood that they will still be installed next month.

The point is not to collect everything. It is to decide what deserves a real place in the setup, what is worth claiming for later and what can be ignored without emotional damage.

Editorial model

Verdict and status are separate

Verdict tells you what we think. Status tells you what is happening. A plugin can be Fire and Free. It can be Nah and Breaking. It can be Stash and Call an Ambulance. These measure different things.

VERDICT

What do we think?

Editorial judgment. Not timing.

Fire
Download and install. Useful, distinctive and likely to remain in the setup.
Stash
Claim it while it is free. Keep it around for a future project if it earns the slot.
Digital Furniture
Functional, but mostly there to occupy disk space and appear in plugin scans.
Nah
Not worth the registration, download, installation or extra scan.

STATUS

What is happening?

Temporal context. Not quality.

Breaking
Fresh news. No pressure. Breaking, not bleeding.
New Release
A recent release. Quality still has to prove itself.
Free
Currently free. Duration not inferred.
Call an Ambulance
A genuinely exceptional opportunity that probably will not wait.
Last Chance
Only when the deadline is documented or officially confirmed.
Updated
An existing article received materially new information.

Evaluation criteria

What gets tested

Usefulness
Does it solve a real production problem?
Workflow
Is it fast, clear and practical?
Character
Does it offer something meaningfully different?
Staying Power
Will it still be installed next month?
