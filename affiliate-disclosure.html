How affiliate links work

When ASP participates in an affiliate program, an eligible link may use an internal /go/ address so the site can maintain one reviewed destination. That address only resolves catalogued HTTPS links and does not change the merchant's price.

Affiliate offers are published only after review, and their disclosure appears on the same page before or near the eligible link.

This means ASP may receive a commission when a visitor makes a purchase through an eligible link, at no additional cost to the visitor.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee positive coverage. Opinions are not for sale, and ASP may criticize a product even when an affiliate relationship exists.

Products are selected and discussed according to their usefulness, value and relevance to the site's audience. Sponsored content, when present, will be identified clearly.

Visitors should buy only what they genuinely intend to use. A lower price does not turn an unnecessary plugin into a sensible purchase.

A discount is not income.

Buying through an affiliate link supports ASP.

Buying something unnecessary does not.
