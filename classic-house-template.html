Eternal Loop Exorcism: The Horizontal Arrangement Coloring Book.

The playbook for a 224-bar classic house arrangement in FL Studio, reproduced page for page. Eight pages, exactly as written.

Why "coloring book"

Because the template ships no samples and no plugins. It is a book of pages drawn in black and white, and you colour them with your own instruments and your own sounds.

Before you trust the automations

The automation clips exist, they are assigned to real parameters, and they move — but they are generic. The sidechain ducking is the clearest case: it reacts to the kick as its source, and how much it ducks depends entirely on what you load into the other tracks. A hard kick and a dense bass need different numbers than a soft kick and a sparse one.

Treat every value in the playbook as a starting point and tune it by ear. Automation that is routed correctly but tuned wrong sounds worse than none, because you stop hearing the problem and start trusting the clip.

Take the template

The project the playbook describes: 124 BPM, 224 bars, twenty-four named tracks — the thirteen elements plus eleven automation rows — with the section markers and the clips already in place. The patterns are empty, because the music is yours.

What it does not contain

No samples, no plugins, no MIDI notes. The patterns are empty placeholders named after their track, and the automation clips sit on a flat default envelope for you to draw. If a pattern looks empty, that is correct — it is a slot, not a loop.
